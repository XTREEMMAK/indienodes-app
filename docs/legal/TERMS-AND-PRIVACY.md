## 1. Scope and short version

- creator-hosted previews cause your browser to contact the creator's media host;
- forms send the information described below to private workflows and reviewers;
- approved creator fields become intentionally public; and
- Turnstile and an activated YouTube trailer contact those providers.

## 2. Who controls the information

Jamaal Ephriam, operating IndieNodes, is responsible for the personal information

## 3. Information handled when you browse

### A. Ordinary request information

When your browser requests a page, script, font, image, public ring file, or embed,
Internet infrastructure necessarily receives information such as IP address, request
time, requested URL, referrer or origin where supplied, browser/user-agent information,
and protocol or security details. IndieNodes does not use this information for behavioral
advertising or build visitor profiles.

The application itself does not create analytics profiles from this information.
Hosting, networking, and security providers may retain limited request or security logs
under their own operational and legal requirements.

### B. Information stored only in your browser

Depending on the features you use, IndieNodes stores the following in browser local
storage:

- liked and “Not for Me” entry IDs;
- discovery-journal events;
- field arrangement, tag filters, theme, skin, display and content preferences;
- player volume, minimized-player position, and ambient-audio acknowledgement;
- a count of visits to IndieNodes on this browser, and a flag recording that the
  one-time feedback prompt has been answered or dismissed; and
- in-progress creator-submission and update drafts.

Submission and update drafts can contain proposed public Node fields. Email addresses and
consent answers are kept in memory and are not intentionally written into those drafts.
Older drafts created by an earlier build may have contained an email address; the current
code drops that address when loading them.

IndieNodes does not receive local-storage data merely because it is stored. You can clear
it in Settings or with browser controls. The export feature downloads selected local data
to a file you control; it excludes form drafts and the viewport-specific player position.
Import reads a file you select and writes recognized values back to the browser.

Files selected in the site generator—including artwork, audio, writing, game assets, and
site content—are processed in the browser to create the preview and downloadable ZIP.
They are not uploaded to IndieNodes by the generator. You decide where to host the
exported site and files.

### C. Creator-hosted media and outside links

Node images, audio, comic pages, game previews, and some text-sample audio are hosted by
creators or their chosen providers. Loading or playing them makes a direct request from
your device to that host. The host may receive your IP address, user-agent, requested
media URL, request time, and limited referrer/origin information and may apply its own
logging, cookie, and privacy practices. Opening a creator's site has the same outside-site
effect.

## 4. Information handled when you use forms

### A. New creator submissions

The join flow may collect and process:

- a source URL, work type, verification token, verification results, submission ID, and
  timestamps;
- proposed public Node fields such as creator name, description, tags, linked media,
  captions or excerpts, artwork information, content type, and explicit-content flag;
- an email address used for review communication;
- rights confirmations, agreement to the Creator Submission EULA, and, for audio
  submissions, performing-rights-organization disclosures; and
- anti-abuse fields such as a hidden honeypot value and elapsed interaction time.

The workflow checks the submitted source page for the verification token and may perform
security and reachability checks on the URL. A human reviews each completed submission.

### B. Creator updates and removals

An update request may include a Node ID, verification information, amended public fields,
an email address, rights reaffirmation, anti-abuse fields, and a Turnstile token. A
removal request includes the Node ID, verification information, an optional reason,
anti-abuse fields, and a Turnstile token; it does not require an email address.

### C. Contact and content reports

The contact form sends the name, email address, message, and, for a content report, the
Node context included in the message. It also sends anti-abuse fields and may send a
Turnstile token. The form is intended for ordinary correspondence and reports. Do not
include passwords, financial data, government identifiers, health information, or other
unnecessary sensitive information.

The contact intake workflow does not write the message to an IndieNodes database and is
configured not to retain workflow execution records. The delivered notification and any
reply may nevertheless remain in the Operator's notification or email systems until
deleted. Delivered notifications and correspondence are retained only as long as needed
to review the message, reply, maintain ordinary correspondence, protect the Service, or
meet a legal obligation, and are then deleted under the Operator's ordinary mailbox and
notification cleanup practices.

### D. Rate limiting and bot protection

IndieNodes uses honeypot and timing checks. Submission workflows also create a keyed,
one-way value derived from the canonical source URL, with a timestamp, to enforce rate
limits and identify recent repeats without keeping another plain-text copy for that
purpose.

Cloudflare Turnstile and Cloudflare-managed security challenges may protect forms or
traffic when configured. Cloudflare says Turnstile processes signals such as IP address,
TLS fingerprint, user-agent header, site key, and associated origin to distinguish
people from bots and improve Turnstile. Cloudflare applies its own Turnstile Privacy
Addendum. A resulting token may be sent with a form for server-side validation.

### E. App feedback ratings

After a number of visits, IndieNodes may ask once whether you are enjoying the app and
offer an optional link to support development. Answering or dismissing it records a flag
in your browser so it is not shown again.

If you submit a rating, IndieNodes sends the rating value, the time you submitted it, the
application version, and the honeypot and timing values described in Section 4.D. **It
does not send any identifier**: no account, no visitor or session identifier, no device
fingerprint, no address, and nothing about which creators or Nodes you viewed. The rating
is about the application itself and is never attached to a creator, a Node, or a
particular work.

Ratings are delivered to the operator as a notification. IndieNodes may also keep each
rating as a single row recording the rating value, the date it was received, and the
application version — no identifier, no time of day, and nothing that could distinguish
one visitor's rating from another's. That record exists so an overall average or trend can
be read; it is never combined into a profile, used to rank or recommend anything, or used
to change what you are shown. Declining costs nothing and the prompt does not return.

- **Contact messages** are not retained in the intake database or workflow execution
